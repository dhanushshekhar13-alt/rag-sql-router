# Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid leave per calendar year, accrued at 2 days per month.
Up to 8 unused days may be carried forward to the next year; the remainder expire on 31 March.

Sick leave is separate: 10 days per year, and a medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

Parental leave is 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver.
Leave requests must be submitted at least 5 working days in advance, except in emergencies.
