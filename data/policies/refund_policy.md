# Refund and Returns Policy

Customers may return any product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided it is unused and in original packaging.

Electronics have a 15-day return window and must include all accessories. Opened software is not refundable.
Furniture returns require a pickup fee of 10 percent of the product price unless the item arrived damaged.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days after the returned item passes inspection.
Sales representatives may approve refunds up to 500 dollars; larger refunds require manager approval.
